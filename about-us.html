Who We Are

Welcome To Radical Polymers

Established as a Proprietor firm in the year 2013, “Radical Polymers” is a leading Manufacturer of a wide range of Rubber Gaskets, Rubber O-Rings, Rubber Seals, etc. Situated in Ghaziabad (Uttar Pradesh, India), we have constructed a wide and well-functional infrastructural unit that plays an important role in the growth of our company.

We offer these products at reasonable rates and deliver them within the promised time-frame. Under the headship of Mr. Sahil Sharma (Proprietor), we have gained a huge clientele across the nation.

To supply reliable rubber components at reasonable rates and deliver them within the promised time-frame — every single order.

To be the first-choice rubber parts partner for Indian manufacturers, known for quality, flexibility and trust.

Honest pricing, consistent quality, quick response and long-term relationships with every client.
