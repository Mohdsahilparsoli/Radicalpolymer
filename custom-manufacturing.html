Custom Development

When Standard Parts Won’t Do

Many applications need rubber parts that are not available off the shelf — a non-standard O-ring size, a special gasket shape, or a spare part for older machinery.

Our team develops these parts end-to-end: compound selection, mould development, sampling and production.

  • Develop from 2D/3D drawings
  • Reverse-engineer from samples
  • Compound & hardness selection
  • Sample approval before bulk
  • OEM & replacement parts
  • Flexible quantities

Choose The Right Rubber

Not sure which compound fits your application? Here's a quick guide — our team will help you finalise.

MaterialBest ForTemp. Range (approx.)Oil Resistance
NBR (Nitrile)Oil, fuel & hydraulic systems-30°C to 100°C
EPDMWater, steam, weather & outdoor-40°C to 130°C
SiliconeFood-contact, extreme temperatures-60°C to 200°C
Viton (FKM)Chemicals, fuels & high heat-20°C to 200°C
NeopreneRefrigerants, general purpose-35°C to 110°C
