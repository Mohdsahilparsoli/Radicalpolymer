Our Approach

Quality Built Into Every Step

Quality is not a single check at the end. We control it from compound selection through moulding, finishing and packing — so issues are caught early and parts are consistent from batch to batch.

  • Compound verified before production
  • In-process checks during moulding
  • Dimensions checked against drawing
  • Hardness (Shore A) verified
  • Visual inspection for flash & defects
  • Lot-wise packing & labelling
