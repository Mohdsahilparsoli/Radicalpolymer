Material Guide

Choose The Right Rubber

Not sure which compound fits your application? Here's a quick guide — our team will help you finalise.

MaterialBest ForTemp. Range (approx.)Oil Resistance
NBR (Nitrile)Oil, fuel & hydraulic systems-30°C to 100°C
EPDMWater, steam, weather & outdoor-40°C to 130°C
SiliconeFood-contact, extreme temperatures-60°C to 200°C
Viton (FKM)Chemicals, fuels & high heat-20°C to 200°C
NeopreneRefrigerants, general purpose-35°C to 110°C
Natural RubberAnti-vibration, abrasion resistance-50°C to 80°C
